## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the "Night Transit" paradigm—a visionary interface for the year 2100 that balances speculative cyber-physical transit infrastructure with utilitarian legibility. The aesthetic mirrors high-reliability aerospace avionics, luminous municipal control surfaces, and tactical navigation HUDs.

The core audience spans everyday planetary commuters, multimodal logistics dispatchers, and high-speed regional travellers navigating complex subterranean, surface, and sub-orbital networks in low-light environments. The interface evokes steady assurance, chronological precision, and seamless clarity under velocity.

The system merges dark-mode technical minimalism with crisp luminous instrumentation. Surfaces are cast in deep, non-reflective abyss inks, punctuated by laser-sharp 1px luminous containment borders, subtle technical blueprint grid backdrops, and an authoritative, restrained beacon gold (`#FFC629`) reserved strictly for focal actions and time-critical anchors.

## Colors

The color palette is built for rapid recognition across variable atmospheric lighting and ocular stress, complying fully with WCAG AAA 7:1 contrast ratios on critical transit data.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Abyss Void (Canvas Ground):** `#070A0F`
- **Deck Ink (Surface Base):** `#0D1117`

- **HUD Ghost (Subtle Borders & Blueprint Grids):** `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.12)`
- **Luminous Edge (Active Borders):** `rgba(255, 198, 41, 0.45)`

### Primary Accent
- **Beacon Gold:** `#FFC629` — Dedicated to primary confirmations, key route anchors, and user focal actions.

### Multimodal Vectors
- **Bus Coral:** `#FF7A59` (High-capacity rapid ground transit)

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Lexend** across all viewports. Its hyper-engineered glyph clearances, expanded open counters, and proven cognitive readability under rapid scanning make it ideal for high-speed transit telemetry.

All numerical readouts, countdown timers, platform codes, coordinates, and schedule matrices must mandate `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` (lining figures) to prevent structural jitter during continuous temporal refreshes.

Label styles feature expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) and uppercase presentation for telemetry tags, platform badges, and mode labels. Headlines enforce tightened tracking to preserve density and structural cohesion.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a dynamic telemetry grid that scales across transit hub kiosks, holographic vehicular heads-up panels, and pocket transceivers:
- **Mobile (Viewport < 768px):** 4-column fluid matrix, `1rem` (16px) margins, `0.75rem` (12px) gutters.
- **Tablet / Cockpit Console (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` (24px) margins and gutters.
- **Desktop / Station Terminal (>= 1200px):** 12-column layout, max-width `1440px`, centered, `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.5rem` (24px) gutters.

A faint blueprint underlay is rendered in canvas coordinates with 32px repeating isometric grid lines (`rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.04)`), anchoring modular data clusters without visual clutter. Minimum vertical and horizontal target spacing between all interactive controls is strictly 8px.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system rejects heavy organic drop shadows in favor of avionics-inspired optical layering, optical glow, and luminous perimeter conduits:

- **Level 0 (Atmosphere Ground):** Deepest `#070A0F` base canvas with faint vector coordinate lines.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Base Tiles):** Fill `#0D1117`, defined by a crisp 1px solid border of `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.12)`. Zero blur shadow.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Floating Cards / Flight Blocks):** Fill `#151B24`, bordered with 1px `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.22)`. Upon focus or state elevation, the border shifts to the respective mode color with an outer aura: `0px 0px 16px -2px rgba(color, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts, Route Overlays, System Interrupts):** Fill `#1A222D`, 1px solid border in `#FFC629`, supported by an outer spatial drop: `0px 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)` accompanied by a `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture avoids soft pill silhouettes, favoring precise, engineered roundedness that feels ergonomic yet industrial:

- **Cards, Panels, and Wayfinding Blocks:** Exactly `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`).
- **Primary & Secondary Action Buttons:** Exactly `14px` border-radius.
- **Status Pills, Telemetry Chips, and Mode Indicators:** `6px` to `8px` corner radius (`rounded-sm`), delivering clean instrument-cluster modularity.
- **Touch Bounds:** All interactable controls must retain strict rectangular or squircle interactive hit envelopes, avoiding circular truncations that erode touch boundary corners.

## Components

### Action Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Minimum height of `56px` with an ergonomic horizontal padding of `24px`. Background `#FFC629`, foreground `#070A0F` in Lexend Bold (tabular nums). Corner radius is locked to `14px`. Hover and active states deploy an edge aura `0px 0px 20px rgba(255, 198, 41, 0.5)`.
- **Secondary Buttons & Action Anchors:** Minimum height of `48px`. Surface is transparent or `#151B24`, framed with a 1px border of `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.25)` and Lexend Medium `#E5E7EB` text. Active touch states shift the border to `rgba(255, 198, 41, 0.7)`.

### Mode & Status Chips
- Height `32px`, corner radius `6px`. Built with a semi-transparent base tint (`12%` opacity of the respective mode color) and bounded by a 1px border matching the mode color at `40%` opacity.
- Typography: `label-mono-sm` uppercase. The leading edge integrates an autonomous micro-beacon indicator (6px circle) glowing at `100%` mode/status opacity.

### Transit Cards & Telemetry Pods
- Bound by `16px` radius, `#0D1117` base, 1px luminous edge. Contains a dedicated top-right status matrix (e.g., Train teal `#2DD4BF` icon next to `ON TIME` in `#4ADE80`).
- Padding: `20px` internal clearance (`space-lg`). Content sections are divided by 1px rules colored `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.08)`.

### Form & Route Inputs
- Minimum height of `52px`. Surface `#070A0F` inset into a panel `#151B24`. Corner radius `12px`.
- Inactive border: 1px `rgba(184, 194, 217, 0.2)`. Focus border: 1px `#FFC629` paired with an inner box-shadow `inset 0 0 0 1px #FFC629`. Placeholder text rendered in `#B8C2D9` at `60%` opacity.

### Wayfinding Timelines & Nodes
- Multimodal route nodes rendered as dual concentric rings: 12px outer ring (Mode color at `30%` opacity), 6px solid inner beacon. Inter-station lines are 2px solid luminous tracks matching transit modes (e.g., Bus coral `#FF7A59`, Air sky `#7CC4FF`).

### Verification Toggles & Selectors
- Touch target strictly `>= 48px × 48px`. Checkbox frames measure `22px × 22px` with a `4px` corner radius, using a 1.5px border of `#B8C2D9`. Checked state floods with `#FFC629` using `#070A0F` check glyph.
